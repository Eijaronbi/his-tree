HIS-TREE
EXPERIENCE THE PAST · UNDERSTAND THE PRESENT · SHAPE THE FUTURE
HIS-TREE is a browser-based 3D history platform. Each episode reconstructs one
documented moment at human scale — the place, the people who were there, the
objects they touched, and the decision that hung on it. You enter as the
Timekeeper , talk to the cast, inspect the evidence, and then choose: witness what
happened, or rewrite it and live with the consequence.
It is not four stories. It is an episode format built to grow: a reconstructed environment, a
sourced cast, and a decision with two sides. The ﬁrst four branches are playable today —
Timbuktu 1324, Ford's Theatre 1865, Chemin des Dames 1917, Berlin 1989 — and the same
format extends to a burning library, a printing press, a city on a lake, a wrong turn in Sarajevo.

HOW AHOLO LUX3D WAS USED
Lux3D is the character and prop pipeline — core 3D content, not decoration. Every cast
member and every player avatar began as an image→3D generation from period reference
plates plus a written prompt describing era, class, posture and garment.

WHAT IS BUILT
Four playable episodes, each with a sourced cast, interactive props, and a two-sided
decision.
Two modes per episode: witness (primary sources, consequence) and rewrite (divergent
branch, staged aftermath).
World map + avatar picker — choose epoch and cast; the map is the library, not a level
select.
Movement verbs: walk, run, and Loﬁ the Yeti's ﬂoat / superman-ﬂight / ground-impact.
Interface: quiet HUD, journal, subtitles, pause menu, adaptive quality tiers.
Zero build step for the game: plain modules, one static artifact of 87 MB / 449 ﬁles.
ENGINEERING NOTES
Vanilla three.js r169 with ES modules; deterministic world assembly from seeded layout tables
so a chapter is identical on every device. Instanced crowd rendering keeps a full theatre within
a few draw calls on mobile GPUs. Non-rigged Lux3D meshes are posed procedurally (lean,
reach, slump) rather than through a skeleton, which is why the cast reads as alive without any
animation authoring. Quality tiers (?q=low|med|high) trade shadow map size and LOD distance
for frame rate.
VERIFICATION
Static build smoke test: all four chapters booted from the deploy artifact with no page
errors and no 404s.
Vision audits: every build was reviewed frame-by-frame by an image model (seams,
ﬂoating props, empty sets, HUD overlap); each ﬁnding has a ﬁx commit.
Mechanics probes: ﬂoat and ﬂight validated against live state (altitude 0 → 2 m → 7.9 m),
not screenshots alone.
Payload budget: 516 MB source → 87 MB deploy artifact, under the free static-host upload
limit.
HONEST LIMITATIONS

crowd is intentionally instanced and repetitive. Only the ﬁrst four episodes are authored — the
